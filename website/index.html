Engineering

Engineered for Silence & Speed

No bloated frameworks. No subscriptions. No cloud surveillance. Just your habits and your discipline.

~50MB RAM Footprint

Built on native WebKitGTK 4.1 via PyWebView. Consumes one-third the resources of heavy Electron wrappers.

Zero Electron bloat

Local SQLite Persistence

All habit tracking, streaks, and timestamps live inside ~/.local/share/habitflow/habitflow.db. 100% offline, private, and portable.

Zero telemetry

Fluid Drag Week Navigation

Click and drag horizontally across the week columns with realistic physics and damping. Each date accurately preserves historical completion state.

GPU-accelerated physics

Habit Deletion & Management

Direct trash actions on every habit row with safety confirmation dialogs, plus a centralized Manage Habits modal to inspect and clean routines.

Safe deletion safeguards

Icon-First Zen Header

Top center navigation with 4 clean rounded square icons for Habits, Notes, Calendar, and Profile. Pure minimalism without visual clutter.

Distraction-free OS

One-Command Auto Install

Automated installer with centered ASCII logo art, dependency verification, desktop launcher (.desktop) registration, and CLI binary.
